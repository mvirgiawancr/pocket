Questions

Is Pocket a real app?

Not yet. Pocket is a concept I designed and built to show how a round-up savings app could look and feel. The waitlist form works, but nothing is sent anywhere.

Where does the money go?

In the concept, round-ups move to a separate savings pot in your name. You can move them back to your main account at any time.

Can I control how much it saves?

Yes. You can pause round-ups, set a weekly cap, or round up to the next five dollars instead of one.

What happens if my balance is low?

Round-ups pause automatically when your account drops below a limit you choose, so saving never gets in the way of paying bills.
